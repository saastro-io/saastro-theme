## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us improve your experience.

## Essential cookies

These are required for the site to function correctly. They cannot be disabled.

-   **cookie\\\_consent** — stores your cookie preferences (1 year)
-   **cf\\\_clearance** — set by Cloudflare, the provider that protects this site against automated traffic and attacks. It stores the result of security checks so they are not repeated on every page. For this site it serves a security function only: it is not used for measurement or advertising. Cloudflare sets its lifetime. It only appears if the site has that protection enabled.
    

## Analytics cookies

These help us understand how visitors use our site. They are only loaded after you give consent. You can withdraw it at any time (see "Managing your preferences"): when you do, they stop loading and we delete the cookies they left on this site.

### Google Analytics 4

<div data-legal="tracking-ga4"></div>

Google's web analytics service. It measures which pages you visit and how you interact with them. It loads from `googletagmanager.com` and sends measurements to Google servers (`google-analytics.com` domains).

- `_ga` — stores a unique visitor identifier.
- `_ga_` followed by the property identifier — keeps session state.

More information: [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Microsoft Clarity

<div data-legal="tracking-clarity"></div>

A Microsoft tool that records how the site is used: session recordings (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement) and heatmaps. It loads from `clarity.ms`.

- `_clck` — stores a unique visitor identifier.
- `_clsk` — groups the page views of one session.

More information: [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement).

### Cloudflare Web Analytics

<div data-legal="tracking-cloudflare"></div>

Cloudflare's analytics service. It measures which pages are visited and how the site performs. It loads from `static.cloudflareinsights.com` and sends to `cloudflareinsights.com` the page address, the browser engine and version and the operating system version, JavaScript memory usage, load timings, an identifier of that page load and the site token. Like any server, Cloudflare also receives your IP address and connection data.

More information: [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Personalization cookies

These remember your preferences and settings to provide a better experience.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer.
